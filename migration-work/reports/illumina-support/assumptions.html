Recommended EDS architecture

  • One EDS repo for support (or shared with marketing in one program); locale folders /en, /ja-jp, /zh-cn, /ko-kr.
  • Alias hosts 301 at the CDN.
  • Query indexes per locale for downloads, training, updates; index sheets from /configuration records.
  • Edge worker: OIDC session check for gated paths, contact info, SWD/SAP proxy.
  • China: in-China CDN/origin, CN tag configuration.

Confidence

Medium. Template and regional findings are high confidence (regular markup, identical aliases). Authentication, SWD and SAP integrations were observed from outside only.

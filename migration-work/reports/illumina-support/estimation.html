Program note — relationship to the Marketing report

This report is deliberately standalone: it prices everything the Support family needs, including platform items that the Marketing family also needs. Both families run on the same AEM application (illumina-designs), share the global header/footer, MyIllumina ribbon, Launch/OneTrust stack and China hosting.

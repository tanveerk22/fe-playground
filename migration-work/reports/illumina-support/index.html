This report estimates migrating the Illumina support site family to Adobe Edge Delivery Services (EDS): the primary site support.illumina.com, its language sites jp.support.illumina.com, support.illumina.com.cn and /ko-kr/, and the regional domains sapac.support.illumina.com and emea.support.illumina.com. We inventoried 17,950 sitemap entries, deeply analyzed 48 primary pages, classified 350 random URLs by their live AEM template, and compared 120 equivalent pages across regions. The support properties resolve into one implementation (AEM app illumina-support, 12 source templates). SAPAC and EMEA support are domain aliases that return byte-identical HTML, so they need configuration only. Templates and blocks are estimated once; Japan, China and Korea are priced as content migration + translation + QA, plus China delivery. Marketing is covered in a separate companion report.

The headline: support is one legacy AEM implementation (Bootstrap 4, jQuery) served on six hostnames, two of which are pure aliases. Of 3,858 primary URLs, 829 are not pages to migrate: 517 /configuration/ data records, 249 bulletins that already redirect to the knowledge base, and 63 FAQ pages that redirect to support-docs. Another 136 Product Security Portal pages sit behind login.illumina.com. The rest reduce to 10 EDS templates and 29 blocks, with download detail pages making up 39%. Total effort is 3,757 h most likely (2,628–5,353 h, ~469.6 person-days) over 18–22 wks with a team of 6–7. The swing factors are gated content/authentication, the Software Downloads service and the document search servlet.

Key findings

One implementation; two aliases Confirmed
Same AEM app (illumina-support) on every domain. SAPAC and EMEA support serve byte-identical HTML to the primary (19/24 and 22/24) and have no canonical tags. They need domain mapping and redirects, not migration.
829 primary URLs are not pages Confirmed
517 /configuration/* records (webinars, training videos, ~40–90 words each; disallowed in robots) feed listings; 249 bulletins (80% already 301 to knowledge.illumina.com); 63 FAQ pages redirect to support-docs.illumina.com.
Download detail pages dominate Confirmed
1,485 of 3,858 URLs (39%) use summarydownloadspage: a highly regular page (banner, file list, SWD iframe). Fully automatable import.
Gated security content needs an auth decision Confirmed
All 12 sampled Product Security Portal documentation/patch pages 302 to login.illumina.com. EDS has no built-in auth; options are edge auth or leaving these 136 pages on AEM.
Listings run on an AEM search servlet Confirmed
Downloads, training and support-update indexes load from /bin/search/documents.json with DAM tag filters. They must be rebuilt on EDS query indexes.
Japanese and Korean are machine translations Confirmed
MT disclaimer on 19/24 (JP) and 18/22 (KR) compared pages. Translation effort is workflow, UI strings and linguistic QA, not re-translation.
China needs its own delivery path Confirmed
Same templates, but separate China farm, ICP licence, China Launch CDN, api.prd-web.illumina.com.cn; YouTube embeds still present (blocked in China).
Source runtime defects Observed
/bin/contact/info returns 500 on every support page; CoA finder MFE throws script errors; Font Awesome and Inter fonts fail (CORS/404).

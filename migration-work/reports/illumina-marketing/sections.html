Regional differences in detail

SAPAC and EMEA — English regional sites Confirmed
Same templates and classes (similarity 1.00 / 0.99). Content is English, mostly identical to www (EMEA PDP rendered identically). Differences: locale (en-AU / en-GB), AU regulatory footer section, extra regional news and events, EMEA exposes 483 kit-selector state URLs in its sitemap. No translation needed.
Japan Confirmed
Same templates (26/26). 23 of 26 compared pages show the machine-translation disclaimer; home page links to Japan-only properties (illuminakk.co.jp iSchool / iAcademy login) and campaign folders /destination/, /on-domain/ that are disallowed in robots.txt and absent from the sitemap. Fonts loaded cross-domain from www fail CORS.
China Confirmed
Same templates (25/25, similarity 0.96). Served from a separate China publisher/dispatcher farm. Adds ICP 沪ICP备2021026389号 + PSB filing, Weibo, WeChat QR, assets.adoberesources.cn Launch, components.illumina.com.cn, api.prd-web.illumina.com.cn, chinalytics.cn, Baidu. Removes GTM, Facebook, Twitter, Instagram, LinkedIn. Video hub not published (Youku per inventory).
Korea Confirmed
Folder /ko-kr/ on www, same templates (28/28, similarity 0.90: lighter page compositions, e.g. MiSeqDx order page). Machine-translated (24/28). Korean legal footer. Global endpoints resolve under /ko-kr and 404 (ribbon links, search terms, country codes).

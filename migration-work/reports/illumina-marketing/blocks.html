EDS block variants

Every one of the 37 Marketing blocks was checked for variants against the component markers on the 248-page deep sample (158 marketing pages: en-US 85, en-AU 14, en-GB 15, ja-JP 16, zh-CN 15, ko-KR 13). A variant is only counted when the EDS block needs a different style, content model, behaviour or regional configuration. Content-only and language differences are not variants: translated copy, per-locale link lists and legacy-vs-core markup of the same component are handled by content and the import parsers. Variants are priced inside their base block; an estimate changes only where the evidence shows work the base estimate did not include.

Corrections and open points from the variant review

  • Instrument specs is a name / value spec list and becomes the Table (specs) variant instead of a separate block (36 separate implementations, not 37). Observed
  • Youku is not used on any of the 15 sampled China pages; rendered China pages call www.youtube.com, which is blocked in mainland China. The Video block keeps a provider option; whether China needs Youku (or another provider) must be confirmed. Needs validation
  • WeChat / Weibo appear only as footer links on China pages; the article share bar on China pages uses Facebook, LinkedIn and Twitter. The Social share block needs no China variant. Observed
  • Staggered columns (System Detail) and the Workcast embed (Event) were not found in the 158-page sample. Both are kept as style options with no extra effort; confirm they are still in use. Needs validation
  • Legacy (Bootstrap) and Core Components markup of the same component, e.g. show-hide vs cmp-accordion, are one EDS block. The difference is handled by the two import parser sets. Observed

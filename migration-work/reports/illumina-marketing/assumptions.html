Assumptions

  • Content source is Document Authoring (DA) with the standard EDS block model; Universal Editor (xwalk) would add ~10–15% to block effort. Assumption
  • Existing JP/KR machine translations and CN localized content are migrated as-is; no full re-translation. Assumption
  • PDFs remain in DAM/CDN at their current URLs. Assumption
  • MyIllumina APIs (profile, cart) will accept CORS calls from the EDS origins. Needs validation
  • Search keeps Google CSE (or Vertex) behind a serverless proxy; China gets a separate provider. Needs validation
  • Redirected/retired URLs (~7%) are not migrated. Observed
  • Korea (/ko-kr on www) is assumed in scope for content migration with all other languages, migrated as a folder of www and priced as a separate increment. Scope not yet confirmed by Illumina. Needs validation
  • Block variants are variant classes or options of their base block and are priced inside it; only Hero, Tabs, Carousel and Instrument specs changed after the variant review (net +14 h). Assumption
  • Japan campaign folders (/destination, /on-domain) are low volume and use existing templates. Needs validation
  • Kit-selector state URLs (EMEA/JP/CN) become app routes, not authored pages. Assumption

Recommended EDS architecture

  • One EDS code repo for the marketing family; content sources per locale (/en-us, /en-au, /en-gb, /ja-jp, /zh-cn, /ko-kr) mapped to domains via CDN.
  • Shared header/footer/placeholders per locale; metadata-driven regional footers and MT disclaimer.
  • Serverless layer (edge worker) replacing AEM servlets: search, Pardot prefill, contact info, ecommerce config, ribbon links.
  • Query indexes per locale for listings, video, documents, products.
  • China: in-China CDN/origin fronting EDS; China-specific tag and video configuration.

Confidence

Medium. Template, block and regional equivalence findings are high confidence (live census + 140 comparisons). Integration and China estimates are lower confidence because backend contracts (MyIllumina, search, China hosting) were observed from outside only.
